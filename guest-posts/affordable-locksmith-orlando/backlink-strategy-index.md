## Target Site List by Vertical

### Vertical 1 — Consumer Protection / Local News
*Post 01: "How to Spot a Fake Locksmith in Orlando"*

**High-priority targets:**
- Orlando Sentinel consumer section (orlandosentinel.com) — large DA, local relevance
- Click Orlando / WFTV community blog submissions
- Better Business Bureau of Central Florida blog (bbb.org/us/fl)
- Nextdoor Orlando neighborhood groups (not backlinks but brand awareness)
- Florida Consumer Action Network (flcan.org)
- Orange County Consumer Fraud Unit resource pages (contact for resource listing)

**Secondary targets:**
- Neighborhood Watch association websites in Orlando metro
- HOA newsletter guest contributors (Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Winter Park HOAs)
- UCF community safety blog / student publication

**Prospecting tip:** Search Google for `site:*.org "Orlando" "scam" OR "fraud" "locksmith"` — you'll find consumer protection pages that might accept a guest resource.

## Anchor Text Diversity (Do Not Over-Optimize)

The goal is natural-looking anchor text diversity. Do not push every site to use the same anchor.

| Anchor Text | Posts Using It | Target % of Links |
|-------------|---------------|------------------|
| Affordable Locksmith Orlando | 01, 04 | ~25% |
| locksmith in Orlando | 02, 06 | ~25% |
| licensed locksmith in Orlando | 03 | ~15% |
| affordable locksmith in Orlando | 05 | ~15% |
| Affordable Locksmith Orlando (brand) | 06 (alt) | ~10% |
| naked URL (affordablelocksmithorlando.com) | varies | ~10% |

If an editor wants to use different anchor text, accept it — editorial natural variation is a positive signal.
